A Heartfelt Thank You

බොහෝ ස්තූතියි!

To every member of our wonderful community who gave their time, energy, and heart to make Albury Wodonga Awrudu 2026 a truly unforgettable celebration — thank you from the bottom of our hearts.

A special thank you to everyone who arrived early to help set up and decorate, transforming our venue into a vibrant celebration of the New Year, and to those who stayed back to clean up — your dedication and spirit made all the difference.

We are deeply grateful to the generous soul who lent their JBL sound equipment, filling the hall with music and energy that kept the celebration alive all day long.

Our heartfelt appreciation goes to the Sinhala School staff and community for their wonderful support, involvement, and encouragement — this event is as much yours as it is ours.

A delicious Avurudu needs exceptional food, and we are so grateful to Mino Sweets for the traditional kawum and kokis that brought the authentic taste of the New Year to our tables, and to Desi Cravings for the wonderful dinner that kept everyone satisfied and smiling.

We are also deeply grateful to Thilanka Dissanayaka and Chanaka Sampath for their generous donations — your kindness and generosity made a real difference to this event.

Most of all — thank you to each and every one of you who participated and celebrated with us. Awrudu is a celebration of community, and you are our community. May the New Year bring you and your families abundant joy, health, and prosperity.
